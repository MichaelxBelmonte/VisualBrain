VISUAL BRAIN 0.2.56 — MAC

1. Unzip the package and drag Visual Brain.app into Applications BEFORE opening it.
   Opened from Downloads or the unzipped folder, macOS runs it from a read-only
   copy and updates cannot be installed there. The app now tries to move itself
   to Applications, but it is better to do it first.
2. Open it, choose Italiano or English, then select folders to map.
3. Click “Create my map” and wait for indexing.

Node is bundled. No npm, Rust or developer tools are needed.
In a node panel, “Node harness → Install” downloads DeepSeek Harness or Harness²
to your profile. npm and uv are bundled too; each user connects their own
account/provider. Installing does not make model calls.
Requires macOS 13.5 or later. The universal package includes Intel and Apple Silicon.

This build uses a stable self-signed identity, without an Apple certificate. It is
not notarized: macOS may require first-launch approval in System Settings →
Privacy & Security. The signature keeps the same identity across versions; it
does not grant folder access. Do not disable Gatekeeper or other system protections.

The map works without AI. To start agents, separately install and sign in to
Claude Code, Codex or Hermes with your own account.
Your index and team boards stay in ~/Library/Application Support/Visual Brain/.
Logs are in the logs subfolder. Scans read only your selected folders and do not
modify original files. Launched AI agents may modify files according to your
instructions and the permissions of their CLI.

Use More → Settings to change language and folders. File names and file contents
are never translated. macOS may restrict access to some folders; authorize only
the folders you want to index.

Local indexing sends no data to external providers. Using AI agents or semantic
analysis may send context to the providers you configure.

Terminals started in 0.2.17 keep running when you quit, update and reopen the app.
Graph and team APIs are unavailable while the app is closed.
When upgrading from 0.2.16 or earlier for the first time, finish work in the old
sessions before quitting: they cannot be transferred to the new engine.
Restarting the Mac or logging out ends sessions. Personal data stays separate.

FIRST UPGRADE FROM 0.2.44 OR EARLIER
The signing identity changes once. Before updating, finish and close all agent
and terminal sessions. After installation, quit the app with ⌘Q, wait at least
10 seconds, then reopen it from Applications: the old terminal service exits
automatically when empty. Alternatively, after saving and finishing your work,
restart your Mac. macOS may ask again for permission to access selected folders.
Keeping terminals from the old version alive can continue triggering repeated
prompts until this step. The update does not terminate them automatically.
